# P4.3 — HA, resilience and session durability

## Goal
Prove Tenant/Operator BFFs tolerate normal process/node failure and upstream degradation without privilege or state corruption.

## Implement/test
- multi-replica BFF deployment behavior;
- production session store/durability model suitable for replicas;
- rolling restart and abrupt process-loss tests;
- OpenStack CompatibilityOperation reconciliation after restart;
- O3K canonical Operation recovery without local authority;
- upstream timeout, connection loss, 5xx, partial outage and recovery behavior;
- bounded retries with no retry storms or duplicated destructive actions;
- readiness behavior during dependency failure;
- session expiry/revocation behavior across replicas.

## Acceptance
Loss/restart of a BFF replica does not silently log users into the wrong scope, lose authoritative O3K work, falsely complete OpenStack operations or require sticky in-memory correctness.
